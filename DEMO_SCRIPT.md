# 1–2 Minute Demo Script

## Suggested screen recording

**0:00–0:15 — Introduce the experience**

Show the dashboard and say: “This is Aarav, a voice-based Virtual Key Account Manager for a Snapdeal seller. It can understand English, Hindi and Hinglish, while responding in English.”

**0:15–0:40 — Investigate the sales decline**

Start a call and say: “My sales have dropped. Can you investigate and tell me what is happening?”

Show the dashboard updating. Aarav should explain the 32.5% sales and order decline, the unchanged average selling price, the increase in out-of-stock products from 5 to 18, and the cancellation-rate increase from 8% to 14%. Emphasise that stock-out and cancellation reason details are sample data/assumptions.

**0:40–1:00 — Restock approval**

Say: “Prepare a restock task for my top five unavailable products.”

Show the five SKU preview. Click Approve and show the mock action result. Mention that no real Snapdeal inventory is changed.

**1:00–1:15 — WhatsApp summary**

Ask Aarav to prepare a WhatsApp summary. Show the exact message preview and approval. Explain that it is a mock record and no WhatsApp message is sent.

**1:15–1:35 — Follow-up intelligence**

Ask for a follow-up in three days. Approve it. Show the saved follow-up record and click **Test Follow-up Now**. Show the recovery report and explicitly identify it as simulated data.

**1:35–1:45 — Close**

Say: “The prototype combines live voice interaction, business-tool investigation, approval-based actions and follow-up reporting.” End the call by voice.

## Recording tips

- Keep the browser and dashboard visible.
- Use headphones and speak clearly.
- Do not show API keys, `.env`, terminal secrets or personal information.
- Do not claim mock actions are live integrations.
- Keep the final recording between one and two minutes.
